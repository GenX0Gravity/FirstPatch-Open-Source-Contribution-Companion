# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want FirstPatch to be a welcoming place for people making their first
open-source contribution. We pledge to make participation in this project a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, ability,
appearance, background, identity, experience level, or technical viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include:

- being respectful, patient, and welcoming to beginners;
- assuming good intent while asking for clarification;
- giving constructive, specific feedback focused on the work;
- accepting reasonable corrections and differing approaches;
- respecting different levels of experience, communication styles, and time
  zones;
- protecting other people's privacy and not sharing sensitive information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or personal attacks;
- sexualized language or unwanted sexual attention;
- trolling, insulting, or deliberately disruptive comments;
- publishing someone else's private information without permission;
- threats, doxxing, or encouraging any of these behaviors;
- sustained bad-faith participation or repeated disruption of project spaces.

## Scope and enforcement

This Code of Conduct applies in project issues, pull requests, discussions,
commits, and other project spaces when someone is representing FirstPatch.
Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, or
pull requests that violate this policy, and may temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when necessary.

To report a concern, use a private contact method available through the
repository's GitHub project rather than posting sensitive details publicly.
Please include enough context for the maintainers to understand what happened,
when it happened, and where it occurred. Do not put personal or sensitive
information in a public issue.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as the circumstances allow. If a
maintainer is involved in the report, use another available project
maintainer or GitHub's reporting tools.
